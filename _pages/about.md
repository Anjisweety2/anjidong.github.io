# Hi, I'm Anji.

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign** under the supervision of [Alexandru Zaharescu](https://math.illinois.edu/directory/profile/zaharesc). My research lies in analytic number theory, with interests spanning additive problems, exponential sums, prime partitions,  Farey fractions, and automorphic L-functions.

Before coming to Illinois, I completed a B.A. at the **University of California, Berkeley**, double majoring in Mathematics and Data Science, with honors thesis supervised by Richard Borcherds.

Alongside my mathematical research, I am interested in algorithms, machine learning, quantitative modeling, and data-driven methods. 



You can browse my [research publications]({{ base_path }}/research/), view selected [technical projects]({{ base_path }}/projects/), or [download my résumé]({{ base_path }}/files/Anji_Dong_CV.pdf).
