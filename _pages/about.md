# Hi, I'm Anji Dong.

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, where I work in number theory under the supervision of [Alexandru Zaharescu](https://math.illinois.edu/directory/profile/zaharesc). My research is in analytic number theory, with interests spanning additive problems, exponential sums, prime partitions, figurate numbers, Farey fractions, and automorphic $L$-functions.

Before coming to Illinois, I completed a B.A. at the **University of California, Berkeley**, double majoring in Mathematics and Data Science. My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Richard Borcherds, was an exposition of the Kronecker-Weber theorem.

Alongside my mathematical research, I am interested in algorithms, machine learning, quantitative modeling, and data-driven methods. My technical work includes natural-language processing, numerical linear algebra, version-control systems, and industrial process analysis.

## Current highlights

- **Research:** Eight peer-reviewed journal articles and nine additional preprints or works in preparation.
- **Teaching:** Instructor and teaching assistant for undergraduate mathematics at UIUC.
- **Background:** Mathematical analysis, asymptotic methods, Python, Java, SQL, and machine learning.
